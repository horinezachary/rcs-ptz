Cameras

RTMP to ONVIF bridge

Each camera appears on the network as its own ONVIF camera (Profile S): its picture as RTSP (the camera's own H.264, repacketized, never re-encoded), and ONVIF PTZ (continuous move, stop, presets, home) turned into the camera's own protocol. Moves need Allow controls, like the keys.
